# compbio-fair-winners

A catalog of winning high-school research projects in computational biology and in the adjacent fields our own leads live in (cancer genomics and model fidelity; urban evolution, citizen-science data and computer vision on organisms). Built as a reference for the ISEF 2026-27 cycle: ACSEF (Alameda County) to CSEF to Regeneron ISEF, then Regeneron STS.

Every entry links to the primary source it was taken from (Society for Science award lists and abstract pages, STS finalist books, ACSEF/CSEF result pages, press releases). Nothing here is from memory.

Coverage notes:

- ISEF: every Computational Biology and Bioinformatics (CBIO) grand award 2015-2026 (the category started in 2015), every top special award 2014-2026, and the first-place winners of the biology, ecology and ML-adjacent categories. 2020 was cancelled.
- STS: top 10 for 2017-2026, top 3 (or top 9 by track) for 2013-2016, plus the comp-bio and ecology finalists from the 2025 and 2026 Finalist Books.
- ACSEF: every ISEF selection and every CSEF qualifier for 2025 and 2026, from the official recap documents.
- CSEF: Project of the Year and first places for 2024-2026 as reported by Santa Clara and San Diego affiliates and school news. The full state archive (`csef.usc.edu`) blocks automated access; see [`competitions/csef.md`](competitions/csef.md) for how to use it by hand.
- JSHS 2025, Davidson Fellows 2025-2026, Thermo Fisher JIC 2025, Google Science Fair 2011-2019, Siemens 2012-2017, BioGENEius 2024: top winners only.
