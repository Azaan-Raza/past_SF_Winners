# ACSEF (Hitachi Science & Engineering Fair, Alameda County)

Our only ISEF-affiliated fair. ISEF rules allow a student to compete in one affiliated fair (plus a state fair reached through it), so Contra Costa and the Synopsys Championship are not options for Quarry Lane students. Run by an all-volunteer non-profit board; affiliated with Thermo Fisher JIC (middle school), CSEF (state) and Regeneron ISEF. Site: [acsef.org](https://www.acsef.org/).

## 2026 by the numbers (from the official recap)

- 636 students registered from 47 high schools and 32 middle schools; 432 projects approved.
- High school: 265 projects, 211 individual and 54 team. Middle school: 158 projects.
- 232 category judges and 38 special-award judges.
- 7 projects (12 students) went to ISEF 2026; 4 of the 7 were teams. 3 of 7 won at ISEF: Yookta Pandit (Quarry Lane, 9th grade) 3rd in CBIO out of 92 projects; Joshua Wu (Dublin HS) 3rd in Biomedical and Health Sciences; Kumar and Srivastava (American HS) Sigma Xi special award.
- The top 10% of each high school and middle school category, excluding ISEF selections, went to CSEF (12 HS and 13 MS projects in 2026).
- Source: [ACSEF 2026 Recap and 2027 Timeline](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ENdOu5G5k82baMGqoI7k0UZAk0cvZb31KeJ4Qa_ZzGw/mobilebasic).

## 2025

- 4 projects (6 students) went to ISEF 2025 and all four placed: Joshua Wu (Dublin HS) BMED 2nd; Saanvi Gudisay and Shivansh Bansal (Dublin HS, team) CBIO 2nd; Ryka Chopra (Mission San Jose) EAEV 4th; Parekh and Tamboli (Washington HS and American HS, two-school team) ENEV 4th.
- 19 students went to CSEF; 5 first places. Yookta Pandit (then 8th grade, Quarry Lane) took a CSEF 2nd with a public-MRI dyslexia classifier.

## What the record says about our odds

- Team projects are 20% of HS entries but were 57% of the 2026 ISEF delegation and 50% of the 2025 one. Teams are not penalized at ACSEF.
- Computational and AI projects dominate the recent ISEF selections and CSEF qualifier lists (seizure prediction wearables, DNA origami design, uncertainty-aware pancreatic cancer DL, edge-AI smart cane, deep-learning network defense, drone active inference).
- Dublin is already represented at the ISEF podium: Dublin HS twice (2025, 2026), Quarry Lane once (2026), Emerald HS at CSEF (2026).
