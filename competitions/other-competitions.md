# Other competitions

## Google Science Fair (2011-2019, discontinued)

Online, international, ages 13-18. Comp-bio grand prizes: Brittany Wenger (2012, neural network breast-cancer diagnosis as a cloud service) and Eric Chen (2013, computer-aided influenza drug discovery). Olivia Hallisey (2015, Ebola test) also took ISEF BMED 1st the same year. The competition no longer runs, but its winners are still the most-cited examples of "a teenager built a classifier on a public dataset and it mattered." Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Science_Fair).

## Siemens Competition in Math, Science & Technology (ended 2017)

Research-paper competition with individual and team tracks and $100,000 grand prizes. Eric Chen won the 2013 individual prize for the same influenza work that won Google Science Fair 2013 and Intel STS 2014. Adhya and Shriya Beesam won the 2016 team prize with a schizophrenia diagnosis system that placed only 4th in ISEF CBIO the same year. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens_Competition).

## BioGENEius Challenge

Run by the Biotechnology Institute with BIO. Finalists come from state and regional fairs and present to industry judges in three tracks (Healthcare, Sustainability, Environment). 2024 winner: Yifan Ding (Boston Latin), compact gene switch for temporal control of CRISPR editing. Worth entering if the cancer lead develops a therapeutic angle. Source: [2024 winner coverage](https://wewillcure.com/insights/entrepreneurship/funding-and-models/yifan-ding-wins-2024-biogeneius-challenge-sponsored-by-cure).

## Synopsys Championship (Santa Clara County)

The ISEF-affiliated fair next door. Not open to Alameda County students, but its grand-prize list is the closest benchmark for what Bay Area ISEF qualifiers look like: in 2026, 11 grand prizes from Harker, Lynbrook, BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, Homestead, Silver Creek and Santa Clara High. Source: [2026 winner summary](https://science-fair.org/2026-winner-summary/).

## Not catalogued, worth knowing

- **Regeneron ISEF special awards** (sponsor awards from NIH, NASA, AAAS, Sigma Xi, universities). Separate from grand awards; the Kumar and Srivastava Sigma Xi award in 2026 is an example.
- **Stockholm Junior Water Prize, Genius Olympiad, iGEM, MIT THINK Scholars, Conrad Challenge.** Different formats; none replace the ACSEF route.
- **Journal publication.** Several STS top-10 projects were published or posted as preprints. A journal paper is the stated stretch objective of our own project.
