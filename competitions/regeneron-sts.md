# Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS)

The most selective US competition for seniors: over 2,000 applications, 300 scholars ($2,000 each, plus $2,000 to the school), 40 finalists ($25,000 each), top 10 awards from $40,000 to $250,000. Applications open in the summer of senior year and close in November; finalists are announced in January and judged in Washington, DC, in March. Intel sponsored it through 2016 (with Basic Research, Global Good and Innovation tracks); Regeneron since 2017.

For the Class of 2029 that means applying in autumn 2028 with the project as it stands then. STS judges a written paper and the student (interviews cover general science knowledge), and rewards statistical depth and independence more visibly than ISEF category judging does.

## What the comp-bio and ecology winners show

- **Comp bio wins the whole thing.** Neel Moudgal (2023, 1st, RNA structure prediction), Benjy Firester (2018, 1st, spatiotemporal late-blight model), Lillian Petersen (2020, 1st, satellite crop forecasting), Andrew Jin (2015, 1st Global Good, ML on public genomes for adaptive mutations).
- **Public data is fine at the very top.** Petersen, Jin, Hong (2025, 6th, Bayesian trans-mQTL mapping), Cong (2024, 2nd, cancer cell-line metabolism vs expression), Luo (2022, 3rd, RiboBayes on public Ribo-seq) and Chowdhury (2022, 5th) all used public data.
- **Statistics-first projects place.** Hong's Bayesian model, Luo's wavelet/Bayesian method, Lipkovitz's causal panel model (2025, 5th) and Orth's acoustic model (2022, 9th) involved no deep learning.
- **Ecology reaches the top 5.** Logan Lee (2025, 4th, mosquito microbiome for Hawaiian bird conservation), Iris Shen (2026, 3rd, clam cancer as a drug model), Pilacek (2024, 7th), Orth (2022, 9th).
- **Finalists repeat from ISEF.** Jerry Xu (ISEF 2026 CBIO 2nd and STS 2026 5th), Edward Kang (ISEF 2025 BMED 1st and STS 2026 2nd), Amber Luo (ISEF CBIO 3rd and STS 3rd, 2022), Prathik Naidu (ISEF CBIO Best of Category and STS 7th, 2017), Ryka Chopra (ISEF 2025 EAEV 4th from ACSEF, STS 2026 finalist).
