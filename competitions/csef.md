# CSEF (California Science & Engineering Fair)

The state final for grades 6-12, held since 1952, run by the California Science and Engineering Fair Foundation. Through 2020 it was at the California Science Center in Los Angeles; since the pandemic it has moved, and the 2026 fair (the 75th) was at California Lutheran University in Thousand Oaks with 882 participants from 356 schools and 725 projects. Senior Division awardees become eligible to represent California at ISEF, which is our second route to ISEF if ACSEF does not select us directly.

## Categories that matter to us

CSEF senior categories are narrower than ISEF's and change year to year. Recent lists include **Computational Systems: Medical**, **Computational Systems & Analysis** (2024-2025), **Computational Science** (2026), **Medicine & Physiology**, **Biochemistry/Molecular Biology**, **Zoology**, **Plant Biology**, **Earth & Environmental Sciences**, **Environmental Engineering**, **Mathematical Sciences**. A comp-bio cancer project would sit in Computational Systems: Medical or Computational Science; a snail or pigeon project could go to Zoology or a Computational category. Check the current category list when the 2027 handbook is posted.

## How to use the state archive

The full project archive with abstracts since the 1990s is at `csef.usc.edu/History/<YEAR>/` with per-category panel pages at `csef.usc.edu/History/<YEAR>/Panels/S##.html` (S = senior division). The site refuses automated fetches, so open it in a browser. Useful searches: every senior computational-category project 2021-2026, every Alameda County entry, and every zoology or ecology project that used observational data. Add what you find to `data/projects.csv` with `competition = CSEF`.

Sources: [SCVSEFA 2026 CSEF results](https://science-fair.org/2026-california-science-engineering-fair-results/), [Harker 2026 report](https://news.harker.org/harker-students-see-success-at-california-science-and-engineering-fair/), [SCVSEFA 2025](https://science-fair.org/2025-california-science-engineering-fair-results/), [SCVSEFA 2024](https://science-fair.org/2024-california-science-engineering-fair-results/), [GSDSEF 2026](https://www.gsdsef.org/past-results/2026), [ACSEF 2026 recap](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ENdOu5G5k82baMGqoI7k0UZAk0cvZb31KeJ4Qa_ZzGw/mobilebasic), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Science_and_Engineering_Fair).
