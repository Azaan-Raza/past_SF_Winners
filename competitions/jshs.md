# JSHS (Junior Science and Humanities Symposium)

A Department of Defense-sponsored research competition for grades 9-12. Students submit a written paper and present orally at a regional symposium; regional winners go to the national symposium in April (the 63rd was in 2025). Awards are scholarships for oral 1st-3rd in each of eight categories plus poster awards; check jshs.org for current amounts. California has northern and southern regions.

Why it matters for us: it is a paper-plus-talk format (closer to STS than to ISEF's poster interview), it does not count against the one-ISEF-affiliated-fair rule, and the same project can be entered in the same spring as ACSEF and ISEF. Matthew Lo (honeybee AI) won JSHS Environmental Science and ISEF Animal Sciences 1st in 2025; Pragathi Kasani-Akula won JSHS Chemistry and ISEF Biochemistry 1st plus SIYSS; Aakash Bhattacharyya took JSHS Medicine 2nd and ISEF CBIO 4th.

Categories: Biomedical Sciences; Chemistry; Engineering and Technology; Environmental Science; Life and Behavioral Sciences; Math and Computer Science; Medicine and Health; Physical Sciences. A cancer-genomics project fits Biomedical Sciences or Math and Computer Science; snail and pigeon work fits Environmental Science or Life and Behavioral Sciences.

Source for 2025: [NSTA press release](https://www.nsta.org/press-release/exceptional-young-scientists-awarded-scholarships-and-awards-63rd-annual-national). Program site: [jshs.org](https://www.jshs.org/).
