# Thermo Fisher Scientific Junior Innovators Challenge (JIC)

The national middle-school competition (grades 6-8), formerly Broadcom MASTERS, run by Society for Science. Students are nominated by their affiliated fair (ACSEF named 16 middle-school nominees in 2026), apply online, and are cut to 300 semifinalists in September and 30 finalists in October. Top prize: the $25,000 ASCEND Award.

Relevant because the ACSEF pipeline starts here. Yookta Pandit (Quarry Lane) was a 2025 top-30 finalist in 8th grade with a public-MRI dyslexia classifier, then placed 3rd in ISEF CBIO as a 9th grader in 2026. Nine ACSEF nominees reached the 2026 top 300 (Batra, Ghosh, Jakkula, Kaur, Nemani, Soni, Srivats, Venkatesh). Ronika Pillarisetty (Quarry Lane, 7th grade) placed 4th at CSEF 2026 with photo-based jaundice detection.

Sources: [2025 top awards](https://www.societyforscience.org/blog/thermo-fisher-jic-top-awards-2025/), [Yookta Pandit finalist profile](https://www.societyforscience.org/jic/2025-student-finalists/yookta-pandit/), [ACSEF 2026 recap](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ENdOu5G5k82baMGqoI7k0UZAk0cvZb31KeJ4Qa_ZzGw/mobilebasic).
