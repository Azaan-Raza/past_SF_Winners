# Davidson Fellows Scholarship

Run by the Davidson Institute for students 18 and under who have completed a significant piece of work in Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, Literature, Music or Philosophy. Awards are $100,000 (Laureate), $50,000 and $25,000; applications are typically due in February (confirm on the program site) and fellows are announced in late summer. It is an application, not a fair: a project paper, nominator letters and supporting material. Many fellows submit the same work they took to ISEF or STS.

Signals from 2025-2026 relevant to us:

- Comp-bio and ecology work from our catalog shows up here a year after the fairs: Amy Xiao ($50,000, 2025; ISEF 2024 BCHM 1st; STS 2025 finalist), Mythreya Dharani ($25,000, 2025; STS 2026 finalist with cell-line-to-tumor drug response), Finnegan McGill ($25,000, 2025; bird acoustics; STS 2026 finalist), Tanishka Aglave ($50,000, 2026; citrus greening with a leaf-photo severity app; ISEF 2024 Horvitz Prize).
- Competitions disagree. Matteo Paz won STS 2025 ($250,000) and received only an Honorable Mention from Davidson the same year. Enter several.
- 2026 Laureate: Aditya Sengupta, ForeCAT, ML forecasting of clear-air turbulence, $100,000.
