# Computer vision and imaging

Image classification, segmentation and quantification on organisms, tissue, cells, scans and field photos. Includes histopathology and medical imaging because the methods and the judging questions (held-out test sets, class imbalance, label quality, domain shift) are the same ones a snail-shell or pigeon-plumage classifier will face.
