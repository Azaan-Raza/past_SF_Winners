# Cancer genomics and model fidelity

Everything in the catalog that bears on PD1-CAN-01: whether gene dependencies and biomarker-dependency links found in 2D cancer cell lines carry over to patient-derived organoids, and more broadly cancer analysis on public data (TCGA, DepMap/CCLE, GDSC, GEO).

## The closest precedents, in order of closeness

1. **Leo Shen, ISEF 2022 CBIO 4th.** "Evaluation of Patient-Derived Xenograft Model Accuracy Using Differential Gene Expression Analysis for Cancer Modeling Optimization." Same question shape as ours (does model X represent model Y?), asked of PDX vs patient colorectal tumors with open RNA-seq. It stopped at descriptive DGE, GSEA and PCA and placed 4th. The gap it leaves open is a prespecified replication statistic, a library-controlled comparison and a second independent dataset. That is our project.
2. **Mythreya Dharani, STS 2026 finalist and Davidson 2025.** I2-CISSP: trains on published cisplatin-tested cancer-cell data, predicts response in patient tumors, and reports which genes drive the prediction. The cell-line-to-patient transfer problem, attacked from the prediction side.
3. **Jonathan Yan, STS 2026 finalist.** Integrated energy and nutrient-use data with metastasis status across 479 human cancer cell lines (CCLE/DepMap scale), found the transsulfuration pathway, then blocked it in cells and the cells stopped spreading. Computation, then one wet validation.
4. **Thomas Cong, STS 2024 2nd ($175,000).** Tested the field's assumption that metabolism follows gene expression across cancer cell lines; immune-related cancers broke the pattern. A public cell-line multi-omics analysis framed as a test of a belief.
5. **CANDraGAT team, ISEF 2022 CBIO 1st.** Graph attention network for cancer drug response from cell-line pharmacogenomics. Shows GDSC/CCLE-type data is a proven 1st-place substrate.
6. **Kevin Zhu, ISEF CBIO 1st 2022 and 2023.** Pan-cancer analysis of short tandem repeats in 10 cancers from public genomes. One neglected variant class, two years, two firsts.
7. **Emma Lee Wen, STS 2025 finalist.** PDAC organoids and spheroids from the wet-lab side; organoids are a model STS judges already know.
8. **Iris Shen, STS 2026 3rd ($150,000).** A clam with a natural blood cancer as a leukemia drug model: a model-fidelity question answered experimentally.

## What the record says

- Public-data cancer analysis alone tends to land 3rd-4th in ISEF CBIO (Dixit 2026, Holzer 2021, Liu 2021, Rahman 2023, Ding 2023, Anisetti 2021 no award). The 1st and 2nd places add one of: a new method with benchmarks (CANDraGAT, EGFRNet), a sharp mechanistic claim (Sahota chromoplexy 2024, Fu resistance dynamics 2026), a neglected signal (Zhu STRs), or a wet validation (Xiao, Yan, Kang).
- The explicit "cell lines vs patients" argument has been made both ways in winning abstracts: Mandera (ISEF 2020) chose TCGA patients over cell lines and said so; Dharani trained on cell lines and transferred. Judges reward the argument being made, with evidence.
- Cancer is the most crowded subject in CBIO. The 2014-2023 ISEF dataset analysis by William Kaiser found projects mentioning cancer or tumors won 10-20% fewer awards in recent years (see `resources/links.md`). Our edge has to be the question (replication across two independent 2026 biobanks) and the design (library-controlled), not the subject.
- The Broad 3D DepMap paper (Neiswender et al., Nature, Aug 2026) and the Sanger organoid biobank paper (Herranz-Ors et al., Nature, Aug 2026) are both from this year. No high-school project in this catalog has used either. See `resources/key-papers.md`.
